# Voice-of-Customer Harvesting Sheet

> **Method:** Copyhackers pain mining — swipe copy from real customer language.

| Source (forums / app reviews / support) | Raw VoC quote | Underlying pain | High-converting redesigned copy |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Community forum | "Failed three times, stuck at 5.5 speaking — the examiner said 'fluency' but I have no idea what to fix at home." | Stuck at a band, desperate, no idea which error to fix first | **"Find the exact 3 errors keeping your band stuck"** |
| App-store review | "A chatbot graded my essay 7.0 with compliments — the real exam tore my pronunciation apart." | Distrust of flattering generic AI; no real-examiner benchmark | **"Diagnosis calibrated on millions of real test submissions — not a chatbot's guess"** |
| Support ticket | "My exam is in 2 weeks; teacher feedback takes 3–4 days, I can't iterate in time." | Time pressure; needs instant feedback loops | **"Take a speaking test. Get your diagnosis in 30 seconds, with per-sentence fixes"** |
| Self-study group | "Center prices run to eight digits; students like me can't afford 1:1 tutoring." | Cost barrier of traditional courses | **"Examiner-standard practice at pocket price per graded attempt — cheaper than one exam retake"** |
